    About Bikolé

    Bikolé helps people in and from Catanduanes understand each other across language lines. Everyday translators struggle with local Bikol words and grammar, so Bikolé uses Meta’s NLLB-200 (600M) model, fine-tuned with LoRA on about 1,500 sentences written in English, Virac and Pandan.

    • eng

      English

      Used in school, work and official communication.

      “Look at this.”

    • bcl_South

      Virac Southern Bikol

      Spoken in Virac and southern Catanduanes.

      “Hilingon mo ini.”

    • bcl_North

      Pandan Northern Bikol

      Spoken in Pandan and northern Catanduanes.

      “Kitaa daw to.”
